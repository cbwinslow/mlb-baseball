# Code of conduct

We want this to be a respectful, constructive place for baseball research and
software collaboration. Be kind, assume good intent, give technical feedback
on the work rather than the person, and welcome people with different levels of
experience.

Harassment, personal attacks, discrimination, threats, doxxing, or deliberately
disruptive behavior are not welcome. The maintainer may remove content or limit
participation when needed to protect the community and project.

Report conduct concerns privately through the maintainer's GitHub profile. Do
not include private information in public reports.
