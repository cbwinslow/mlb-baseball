  [GitHub issue #267](https://github.com/cbwinslow/mlb-baseball/issues/267);

  `csv_batting`), always in pairs: one player short a plate appearance/
  strikeout/walk/home run, another player with exactly the same extra one, in
  the same game. **Root cause, confirmed by reading the actual event rows (see
  below): a real bug in this change's own gate SQL**
  (`mlb_baseball/sql/tieout_player_event.sql`), not a data problem. **Fixed in

### Investigating the run-count difference (issue #267)

```sql
select event_id, bat_id, event_tx, event_runs_ct, away_score_ct
from raw.retrosheet_event where game_id='BOS202509030' and event_id::int between 13 and 19
order by event_id::int;

 event_id | bat_id   | event_tx                       | event_runs_ct | away_score_ct
 13       | roccb001 | S8/G4M.2-3                     | 0             | 3
 14       | kwans001 | S7/G56.3-H;1-2                 | 1             | 3
 15       | marta008 | 8/F89XD.2-3;1-2                | 0             | 4
 16       | ramij003 | C/E2/OBS/G2-.3-H(RBI);2-3;B-1  | 0             | 4   <-- should be 1
 17       | manzk001 | WP.3-H(NR);1-2                 | 1             | 4
 18       | manzk001 | S7/L7.2-H(UR);BX2(74)          | 1             | 5
 19       | noelj001 | 53/G5S                         | 0             | 6
```

Event 16's own text says `3-H(RBI)` (the runner from third scores, with an
RBI), but `event_runs_ct` is 0 for that row, and `away_score_ct` (Chadwick's
own running score, confirmed to mean the score *entering* each play by cross-
checking the surrounding rows) never reflects that run anywhere later in the
game either -- the whole game's `event_runs_ct` sums to 8, one short of the
independently-parsed CSV product's 9 (`raw.retrosheet_plays`/
`raw.retrosheet_batting`, which do count `run3 = roccb001`, `runs = 1` on the
same play). `cwevent` appears not to apply the `3-H` advance to its run tally
for this specific play type (obstruction combined with a fielding error).
Full detail and the CSV-side confirmation query are in issue #267.

### Investigating the player-game failures (gate bug, now fixed)

Four real examples from the first run (one per game, of ~250):

| Season | Game | Players (event extra / csv extra) |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 2015 | `CIN201509250` | `campe001` / `uribj002` |
| 2015 | `KCA201509030` | `romia001` / `iglej001` |
| 2015 | `SFN201506160` | `parkj002` / `pagaa001` |
| 2015 | `WAS201507220` | `uggld001` / `escoy001` |

Reading the raw event rows for the first pair:

```sql
select event_id, bat_id, resp_bat_id, event_cd, bat_event_fl, event_tx
from raw.retrosheet_event
where game_id='CIN201509250' and (bat_id in ('campe001','uribj002') or resp_bat_id in ('campe001','uribj002'))
order by event_id::int;

 event_id | bat_id   | resp_bat_id | event_cd | bat_event_fl | event_tx
 62       | campe001 | uribj002    | 3        | T            | K
```

`bat_id` (literally at the plate for this specific event row) is `campe001`;
`resp_bat_id` (the batter Retrosheet's own convention credits the statistical
result to, for an in-progress substitution such as an injury or ejection) is
`uribj002`. `tieout_player_event.sql` grouped by `bat_id`; the CSV product's
`batter`/`id` columns (`tieout_player_csv_plays.sql`/
`tieout_player_csv_batting.sql`) already use the credited batter. The other
three examples above reproduce the exact same mechanism (checked the same
way). This project's own `pitching_game_build.sql` already gets this right
for pitchers (groups by `resp_pit_id`, cites Chadwick's docs) -- the batting
side of the *gate* just hadn't been given the same treatment. A similarly-
caused bug was also found in **production's** `gold.batting_game` (built by
`mlb_baseball/sql/batting_game_build.sql`, unrelated to this gate) -- out of
this change's scope to fix, filed as
[GitHub issue #266](https://github.com/cbwinslow/mlb-baseball/issues/266).

**Fix applied to the gate** (`mlb_baseball/sql/tieout_player_event.sql`):
group by `resp_bat_id` instead of `bat_id`. `tests/integration/
test_tieout_run.py`'s fixture now includes a mid-plate-appearance substitution
case (`ccsub001`/`ccccc001`) and two tests: one proving the fix passes it
correctly, one proving the check still fails if grouped by the wrong id
(`test_a_mid_plate_appearance_substitution_is_credited_to_the_responsible_batter`,
`test_crediting_the_substitute_instead_of_the_responsible_batter_fails`).
